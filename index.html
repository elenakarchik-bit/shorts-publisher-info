Telegram YouTube Test

A personal video publishing tool operated by the owner of this GitHub account. It transfers the operator's selected videos and captions from a Telegram topic to a YouTube channel authorized by the operator.

The tool creates a short title from the original caption and uploads the video through the official YouTube Data API. Private uploads are used for testing. Scheduled public uploads will only be enabled after configuration by the operator.

This application is for the operator's personal use. It is not a public service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram, Google, or YouTube.
